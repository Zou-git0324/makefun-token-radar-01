How the value is worked out radar-2

implied = TVL × 0.20 × category × growth × maturity × chain breadth

  • TVL × 0.20 — the size of the business a token would sit on. Category is a risk haircut, now set by the market as well as by hand: each category’s multiplier starts from the hand-set table (curators and lenders 1.05–1.15, wrappers and stable shells 0.80–0.90) and moves by up to a quarter from the median market-cap/TVL of that category’s live tokens in this same snapshot — only where at least 3 live tokens price it. The panel below shows the evidence per category.
  • Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, weighted 0.6. A protocol still compounding scores above one that is bleeding out.
  • Maturity — 0.65 + 0.35 × min(age, 4 years) / 4 years. Holding a large TVL for years with no token is pent-up launch value; the curve flattens after four years.
  • Chain breadth — +2% per extra chain, up to +8%. Multi-chain protocols are less exposed to one chain’s outage or incentive cut.

“Records no token” means the snapshot carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it. That is the record, not proof — a protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. Implied value is this radar’s own claim, not a prediction that a token launches, or when.
